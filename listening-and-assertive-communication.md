# Listening and Active Communication

#### Question 1

###### What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

Steps/Strategies to Follow Active Listening:

- 1. **Listen carefully:** Pay full attention to what the other person is saying and try to understand their message.

- 2. **Avoid distractions:** Keep away from mobile phones, other thoughts, and anything that takes your attention away from the conversation.

- 3. **Focus on the speaker:** Give your full attention to the person who is speaking.

- 4. **Do not interrupt:** Allow the speaker to share their thoughts without stopping them in the middle.

- 5. **Let the speaker finish:** Wait until the other person completes their point before giving your response.

- 6. **Use door openers:** Use phrases like "Can you tell me more?" or "I would like to know more" to encourage the speaker to continue.

- 7. **Use body language:** Maintain eye contact, nod your head, and show interest through your facial expressions.

#### Question 2


###### According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

-  **Listen carefully**: Pay full attention to the speaker and understand what they are trying to say.

-  **Understand their feelings:** Try to understand the speaker's emotions and feelings, not just their words.

-  **Repeat in your own words:** Explain what you understood using your own simple words.

-  **Confirm your understanding:** Ask the speaker whether you understood their message correctly.

- **Show empathy:** Try to understand the situation from the speaker's point of view without judging them.

- **Ask questions:** Ask questions to get more information and clear any doubts.

- **Do not interrupt or judge:** Allow the speaker to express their thoughts and feelings freely.

- **Give proper feedback:** Respond in a way that shows you understand and care about what the speaker is saying.

#### Question 5

###### When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?
  I switch to a passive communication style in my daily life when:
- I feel nervous or shy to express my opinions.

- I avoid arguments or conflicts with others.

- I am afraid of hurting someone's feelings.

- I agree with others even when I have a different opinion.

- I hesitate to ask questions when I have doubts.

- I find it difficult to say "No" when someone asks me for help.

#### Question 6

###### When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

I switch to an aggressive communication style in my daily life when:
- I get angry or frustrated during a conversation.
- Someone does not listen to my opinions.
- I feel that someone is blaming me unfairly.
- I lose my patience when things do not go as expected.
- Someone repeatedly makes the same mistake.
- I try to prove that my opinion is correct without listening to others.

#### Question 7

###### When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

I switch to a passive-aggressive communication style in my daily life when:
- I feel hurt but do not express my feelings directly.

- I use sarcasm instead of openly saying what is bothering me.

- I give silent treatment when I am angry with someone.

- I make indirect comments or taunts when someone disappoints me.

- I avoid talking about problems instead of discussing them openly.

- I sometimes complain about someone to others instead of speaking directly to that person.
