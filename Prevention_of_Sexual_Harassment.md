# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

#### Question 1

###### 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any unwanted sexual behaviour that makes a person feel uncomfortable, unsafe, threatened, or disrespected. Examples include:

- Unwanted physical contact: Touching, hugging, or brushing against someone without consent.
- Verbal harassment: Making sexual comments, inappropriate jokes, or remarks about someone's body or appearance.
- Non-verbal harassment: Staring, making inappropriate gestures, or showing sexually explicit images.
- Online harassment: Sending unwanted sexual messages, photos, videos, or inappropriate content through social media or email.
- Repeated unwanted attention: Continuously asking for dates or making unwanted advances even after being told no.
- Workplace harassment: Offering job benefits or promotions in exchange for sexual favours or threatening someone's career for rejecting such advances.

#### Question 2

###### 2. What would you do if you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face or witness sexual harassment, I would take the following steps:

1. Stay calm and act firmly: If it is safe, clearly communicate that the behaviour is inappropriate and must stop.
2. Support the affected person: Listen to them, respect their feelings, and avoid blaming or judging them.
3. Document the incident: Keep records of dates, times, locations, messages, emails, and other relevant evidence.
4. Report the incident: Inform the HR department, manager, or Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) through the appropriate workplace reporting process.
5. Maintain confidentiality: Avoid spreading information about the incident and respect the privacy of the affected person.
6. Follow up: Ensure that appropriate action is taken and that the affected person receives support and protection from retaliation.

My responsibility: I believe everyone deserves a safe, respectful, and inclusive workplace. I would never ignore sexual harassment, whether I experience it directly or witness it happening to someone else.
