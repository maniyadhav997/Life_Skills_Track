#### Question 2

###### Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

The video by Trevor Ragan explains **Growth Mindset**, a concept introduced by psychologist Carol Dweck.

- **Fixed Mindset:** Believing that skills and intelligence cannot change. People avoid challenges and fear failure.
- **Growth Mindset:** Believing that skills can improve through hard work and practice.

**Four key things for growth:** Effort, challenges, mistakes, and feedback.

**Key Takeaway:** We should accept challenges, learn from mistakes, take feedback positively, and keep improving. A person's mindset can change depending on the situation.

#### Question 3

###### What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

**Internal Locus of Control** means believing that you have control over your life and that your actions, efforts, and decisions influence your results.

- **Motivation:** When you believe your actions lead to success, you stay motivated and keep trying even when things are difficult.
- **External Locus of Control:** People believe their results depend on luck, other people, or situations. They may give up because they feel they cannot change the outcome.
- **How to Develop It:** Solve your own problems, take responsibility for your actions, and recognize how your efforts bring positive changes.

**Key Takeaway:** Don't blame luck or circumstances for everything. Focus on what you can control and take responsibility for your actions.
