## Question 1:
###### What is the Feynman Technique?

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you explain a concept in simple words, as if teaching a beginner, to identify and fill gaps in your understanding.

## Question 3:
###### What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

##### Two fundamentally different modes of thinking to facilitate learning:

**Focused Mode:** This is the state of concentrated attention. It is used when you are working on something familiar, applying logical, sequential patterns, or processing information you have learned before. Think of this as the "tight" setting in a pinball machine, where the ball travels along established pathways.

**Diffuse Mode:** This is a relaxed state of resting neural connections. It allows your brain to wander and explore, making new, creative connections between distant ideas. This mode is essential when you are trying to understand a concept that is entirely new or when you get stuck while focusing; by stepping away from a problem, you allow this mode to work on it in the background.


## Question 4:

###### According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.

**Deconstruct the skill:** Break the skill down into smaller, manageable parts and identify the most important sub-skills to practice first.
**Learn enough to self-correct:** Gather just enough resources (3-5) to identify mistakes while practicing, rather than using research as a form of procrastination.
**Remove barriers to practice:** Use willpower to eliminate distractions, such as the internet or television, to ensure you actually sit down to practice.
**Practice for at least 20 hours:** Pre-commit to 20 hours of focused practice to overcome the initial "frustration barrier" and feel competent.
